    About this prototype & sources

    Original browser/debug prototype. Controller input is experimental. Select your controller type. Switch Pro wired motion and button layout need a physical check; this does not claim exact Splatoon response. Mouse and keyboard remain available.

    Rendered with Three.js (MIT). Architecture informed by the official HOJA3 app. Weapon presets use community-extracted version 11.3 parameters; Splattershot cadence/startup are provisional trainer settings; full game physics are not simulated. Research sources.
